Frailty–Inflammation Phenotype Calculator

A lightweight web-based clinical calculator for identifying inflammation-associated frailty phenotypes in older surgical patients.

What this tool does

This calculator integrates:

Systemic inflammatory burden
Fried frailty phenotype components

to automatically classify patients into four clinically meaningful phenotypes:

Low inflammation – Low frailty
Low inflammation – High frailty
High inflammation – Low frailty
High inflammation – High frailty

The tool is designed to help identify patients with the inflammation-associated frailty phenotype, a potentially high-risk subgroup relevant to perioperative management and prehabilitation research.

Key Features
Raw clinical input only

Users enter routine clinical variables directly:

Neutrophil count
Lymphocyte count
Platelet count
Height and weight
Grip strength
Walking time
Physical activity
Fatigue status

The calculator automatically computes:

BMI
NLR
SII
Fried frailty score
Inflammatory burden score
Final phenotype classification

No manual calculation is required.

Dual-axis phenotype visualization

The tool provides an intuitive two-dimensional phenotype map integrating:

Frailty burden
Inflammatory burden

allowing rapid visual interpretation of patient subtype.

Research-oriented design

The calculator was developed to support:

Frailty research
Perioperative risk stratification
Prehabilitation studies
Inflammation-related aging research
Precision perioperative intervention strategies
